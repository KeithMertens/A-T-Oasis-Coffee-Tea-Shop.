About Coffee & Beyond

Your Industrial Loft Coffee Haven in Dasmariñas

Situated along Green Breeze Avenue in Langkaan I, Dasmariñas, Coffee and Beyond was designed to offer students, remote workers, and food lovers a stylish, loft-inspired retreat.

From rich Tiramisu iced coffees and soothing matcha lattes to sizzling sisig, truffle pastas, and fluffy ice cream waffles—our menu goes far beyond ordinary coffee. Whether you're pulling a late-night study grind or hanging out with your favorite crew, we've got a warm table waiting for you.
