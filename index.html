About A.T. Oasis

Rooted in East African Tradition & Passionate Craftsmanship

Located at 4613 E Thomas Road in Phoenix, A.T. Oasis Coffee & Tea Shop specializes in hand-selected, all-natural beans sourced directly from small farmers in Ethiopia and Eritrea.

Founded with love by Aisha and her dedicated team, we pour our heart and soul into every cup. From our trade-secret A.T. Ginger Coffee and scratch-made organic chai teas to vegetable sambusas and savory breakfast items, every visit is a warm cultural experience.

Specialty Origin Roasts

Ethiopian Yirgacheffe, Sidamo, and exclusive spice blends.
